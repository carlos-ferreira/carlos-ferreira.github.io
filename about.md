I'm Carlos Ferreira, a software engineer based in Portugal. I write about software development and architecture through the projects I work on. I like the point where a design meets everyday use and someone does something perfectly reasonable that I hadn't thought to test.

This blog is where I work through questions that come up when I'm building software. Sometimes that means tracing a bug back to an assumption I made. Other times it means revisiting a design decision once I've had to live with it for a while.

You can explore my projects on [GitHub](https://github.com/carlos-ferreira) or find out more about my professional background and get in touch on [LinkedIn](https://www.linkedin.com/in/carlos-ferreira-developer/).
